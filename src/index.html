01About

I'm a full-stack software engineer in Dhaka, Bangladesh with a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from BUET. Most of my work lives on the backend: designing APIs, modelling data in PostgreSQL, wiring third-party integrations, and keeping deployments boring with Docker and GitHub Actions.

Increasingly that backend work is AI engineering—async document ingestion, embeddings and semantic search with pgvector, LangGraph workflows, and LLM-powered evaluation with real-time progress streamed to the UI. I treat LLM features like any other service: queued, observable, and measured.

I care about performance I can prove—profiling before optimizing, fixing N+1 queries, and watching p95 rather than averages. Before industry I did research in machine learning and systems, including a co-first-authored CHI EA '25 paper.

    Co-first author. Built a Whisper + Pyannote pipeline for Bangla-to-English transcription with speaker diarization, and a RoBERTa classifier fine-tuned on GoEmotions to quantify 27 emotions across interviews.

  2. arXiv

    Align Where the Words Look: Cross-Attention-Guided Patch Alignment with Contrastive and Transport Regularization for Bengali Captioning

    Frozen MaxViT encoder + mBART-50 decoder trained on a single GPU with a 110k-image synthetic corpus; state-of-the-art low-resource results (BLEU-4 12.29) and a 41% smaller real–synthetic domain gap. Under review at PRICAI 2026.

    Neural Prefetch Filtering for Microarchitectural Prediction

    Replaced a perceptron prefetch filter (ISCA 2019) with a feed-forward neural filter in C++/ChampSim, cutting model weight storage by 93% at under 1% IPC loss. Funded by a BUET RISE research grant.
